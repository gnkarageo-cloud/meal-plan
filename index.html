      Miso-honey dressing
      Ingredients
      • 2 tbsp white miso paste
      • 1 tbsp honey
      • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
      • 2 tbsp olive oil
      • 1 tbsp lemon juice
      • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger (optional)
      Instructions
      1. Add all ingredients to a clean jar.
      2. Shake or whisk until miso is fully dissolved.
      3. Store in fridge — lasts the full week.
      Herby kefir sauce
      Ingredients
      • 300g kefir
      • 2 garlic cloves (minced)
      • Large handful fresh parsley
      • Juice of 1 lemon
      • Salt to taste
      Instructions
      1. Whisk or blend all ingredients together.
      2. Store in a jar in the fridge.

      Banana oat bread
      Ingredients (double batch)
      • 3 very ripe bananas, almost black (mashed)
      • 150g rolled oats (blended into flour)
      • 2 whole eggs
      • 3 tbsp natural peanut butter
      • 2 tbsp honey
      • 1 tsp vanilla extract
      • 1 tsp cinnamon
      • 1 tsp baking powder
      • Pinch of salt
      • 30g walnuts (optional)
      • 30g dark chocolate chips 85%+ (optional)
      Instructions
      1. Mash bananas in a large bowl.
      2. Blend oats into flour.
      3. Mix all ingredients together until combined.
      4. Pour batter into a lined loaf tin. Bake at 175°C for 35–40 min.
      5. Cool completely before slicing into 8–10 pieces. Store in fridge.
      Makes 2 loaves — bake separately
